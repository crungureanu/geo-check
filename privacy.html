Privacy Policy

Short version: no accounts, no tracking, no marketing. We keep only what is needed to run and protect the scanner.

Last updated: 18 May 2026

Who we are

XEOscan is a free tool operated by Constantin Ungureanu. You can reach us through our contact page.

What we collect

  • The URL you submit and the pages we fetch from it to produce a report.
  • Scan results, stored so a share link works. These auto-expire after 7 days.
  • Scan log: the scanned URL and a timestamp, for abuse prevention and aggregate usage statistics.
  • Limited request metadata (such as IP address) used transiently by our host for rate limiting and bot protection.
  • Contact form data you choose to send us: your name, email and message, used only to reply to you.

We do not require an account, we do not ask for payment, and we do not send marketing email.

Cookies

We use a single strictly-necessary cookie set by Cloudflare Turnstile to tell humans from automated abuse. We do not use analytics, advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. You can accept or reject non-essential cookies in the banner shown on your first visit; today there are no non-essential cookies to reject.

Third parties

  • Cloudflare: hosting, storage (Workers KV) and Turnstile bot protection.
  • Google PageSpeed Insights: we send the scanned URL to Google to fetch Core Web Vitals. This is subject to Google's privacy policy.

We do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

Retention

Share reports and their data are deleted automatically 7 days after a scan. Scan-log entries are kept for a limited period for abuse prevention and statistics, then expire. Contact messages are kept only as long as needed to handle your enquiry.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete a stored report or a contact message at any time via the contact page. Because reports expire on their own within 7 days, most data clears without any action.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
