# Trends Research: Free SEO / GEO / AIO website checks (12-month view)

## Summary
Search demand for **SEO audits/checks** is alive and growing (peak score 100 in late April 2026, up from ~50 a year earlier). Demand for **GEO** or **AIO** as audit/check tool concepts is still **near zero** in Google search. However, the underlying topics powering those acronyms are exploding: **"ai seo"** went from 23 → 100 (peak), and **"generative engine optimization"** went from 1 → 14 (peak). So users are researching the topic, but they aren't yet typing "free GEO check" or "free AIO check" into Google. There's a clear gap between topical curiosity and tool-seeking behaviour.

## Key takeaways

1. **"seo audit"** sustained growth: 53 (May 2025) → 100 (Apr 26 – May 2, 2026). A ~90 % rise year-on-year. Free-tool demand for classic SEO is healthy.
2. **"seo check"** steady but smaller, oscillating 9 – 28. Peak also late April 2026 (28). Slow upward drift.
3. **"geo audit"** sat at 0 for most of the year. Only registered measurable values from late February 2026 onward, peaking at 5. Not a real tool-search term yet.
4. **"aio audit"** essentially zero throughout, peaks at 2. Not a real search term.
5. **"ai seo"** is the breakout: from 23 → peak 100 (Apr 12 – 18, 2026), roughly 4 – 5× growth in 12 months. The dominant AI-era SEO search phrase.
6. **"generative engine optimization"** grew 14× from a tiny base: 1 → peak 14 (Apr 12 – 18, 2026). Real momentum, still niche.
7. All terms cooled in the most recent 2 weeks (early May 2026). The last week is **partial data** and should not be over-interpreted.

## Interpretation for the business

- **People still want a free SEO check.** That demand is intact and gently growing. A free-SEO-check landing page remains a viable lead magnet.
- **"Free GEO check" / "free AIO check" is not yet a search behaviour.** Building a tool branded exactly like that would chase volume that doesn't exist (yet).
- **The opportunity is positioning, not product.** Audiences are researching "ai seo" and "generative engine optimization" but haven't moved to tool-shopping language. A landing page titled around *"AI SEO audit"* or *"check if ChatGPT can find your site"* would catch the rising interest without being keyword-blocked by a niche acronym.
- **Watch the next 3 months.** "ai seo" and "geo" both peaked then dipped. If they recover and break above their April peaks by July, that's the cue to invest more. If they keep cooling, treat them as plateauing niche topics.
