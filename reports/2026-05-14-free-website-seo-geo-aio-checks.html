Headline reading

  • "Free SEO check / audit" demand is real and growing. "seo audit" rose from a baseline of ~50 in May 2025 to a peak of 100 in late April 2026 — roughly a 90 % year-on-year rise.
  • "Free GEO check" is not yet a search behaviour. "geo audit" sat at zero for most of the past year and only registered measurable values from late February 2026 onward, peaking at 5 (vs SEO's 100).
  • "Free AIO check" is essentially noise. "aio audit" hovered at 0 – 2 for the entire 12 months. Not a real keyword.
  • The underlying topic is exploding though. "ai seo" went from 23 → peak 100 in 12 months (4–5×). "Generative engine optimization" went from 1 → peak 14 (14× from a tiny base).
  • The gap is positioning, not demand. Audiences are reading about AI-era SEO. They are not yet searching for tools branded as "GEO check" or "AIO check". A landing page positioned around "AI SEO audit" would catch the rising tide without being blocked by a niche acronym.
  • April 2026 was the peak for every term. All six series peaked between 29 March and 2 May 2026 and then cooled. Watch the next 3 months — if "ai seo" and "geo" break their April peaks by July, the trend has legs; if not, it's plateauing.
