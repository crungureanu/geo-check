Please read this before relying on a scan.

Last updated: 18 May 2026

XEOscan gives an indication of how well a website is prepared for AI assistants and classic search. It is not a guarantee, a certification, or a measurement of how any AI model actually ranks or cites a site.

Results may not be fully accurate

The information in a report may not be 100% accurate or reflect reality. Web pages change, servers behave differently for different visitors, and automated checks can misread unusual markup. Treat every finding as a prompt to investigate, not as a definitive verdict.

We do not scan your whole website

XEOscan samples a limited number of pages (typically around 10), chosen by type, plus root files such as robots.txt, sitemap.xml and llms.txt. Issues on pages we did not fetch will not appear in the report, and patterns we infer from the sample may not hold across every page on your site.

We cannot see how AI ranks you

We do not have visibility into how ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews or any other system actually ranks, retrieves or cites your content. XEOscan only tests the publicly observable signals that make a site ready to be read and cited. A good score does not guarantee citations, traffic, rankings or revenue, and a poor score does not guarantee the opposite.

Not professional advice

Reports are provided for general informational purposes only and do not constitute professional SEO, legal, technical or business advice. You are responsible for any changes you make to your website based on a report. We accept no liability for actions taken, or not taken, in reliance on a scan.

Third-party data

Core Web Vitals are fetched live from Google PageSpeed Insights and are subject to Google's own accuracy and availability. If a third-party service is unavailable, parts of a report may be missing or incomplete.
