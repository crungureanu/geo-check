Google AI Overviews: what they are and how to get included

Google AI Overviews are AI-generated summaries shown above traditional results for some queries, and getting included means having clear, authoritative, well-structured content that Google can confidently summarise and link.

Last updated: 18 May 2026

What Google AI Overviews are

Google AI Overviews are AI-generated summaries that appear above the traditional list of results for some queries, often citing and linking the pages they draw from. This is the surface most people mean when they say AIO. It is distinct from Google AI Mode, which is a separate conversational search experience; this page is about Overviews, and the term comparison lives on GEO vs AEO vs SEO.

How inclusion seems to work

Google does not publish a formula, so treat any specific trigger claim with caution. What is consistent: AI Overviews are built on the same classic-search foundation as ranked results, so a strong, crawlable, well-structured, authoritative page is the prerequisite. Clear answer-shaped content with visible authorship and dates is easier to summarise confidently than vague or unstructured content. Inclusion is not guaranteed or directly controllable.

What to do on your site

  • Keep the classic SEO foundation strong: crawlable, fast, HTTPS, canonical, sitemap.
  • Write answer-shaped sections with clear question headings and self-contained answers.
  • Add structured data that matches the page so the content is machine-readable.
  • Show a named author, a clear publisher and an honest last-updated date.
  • Link out to primary sources where you make a factual claim.

AI Overviews and clicks

When a summary answers the question on the results page, some users will not click through. The practical response is not to chase the summary at all costs but to be the source it summarises and links, and to make the click worthwhile for the readers who do come. This is described qualitatively: there are no reliable public click-through figures to quote here.

FAQ

Is AIO the same as AI Overviews?

AIO (AI Optimization) is a loose label that, in common usage, most often refers to optimising for Google AI Overviews. AI Overviews is the actual Google surface; AIO is the informal term people use for working toward it.

Do AI Overviews replace SEO?

No. AI Overviews sit on top of Google's classic search foundation, so strong, well-structured, authoritative content still matters. It changes how results are presented, not whether quality content is needed.

Can I opt out of AI Overviews?

Any opt-out behaviour is defined by Google's own documentation and can change. This page does not assert a specific control; check Google's current guidance before relying on one.
