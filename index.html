          FAQ

          What is AI SEO?

          Optimising your site so that AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews) can read, understand, and cite your content when users ask them questions. It overlaps heavily with classic SEO but emphasises crawler access for AI bots, structured data, clear authorship, and answer-shaped content.

          Is AI SEO the same as GEO, AEO and AIO?

          Yes, essentially. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization), AEO (Answer Engine Optimization), and AIO are different names the industry uses for the same goal: making your site citable by AI. We use "AI SEO" because it's the term most people search for.

          What does this tool check?

          About 40 signals across eight areas: AI crawler access (robots.txt for GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and 10 others), discoverability (sitemap, llms.txt, canonical, HTTPS), structured data quality (JSON-LD), content extractability (raw HTML word count, headings, alt text), citability (author, dates, outbound links), answer-shape (question headings, FAQ schema, lists), classic SEO basics (title, meta, Core Web Vitals), plus auto-generated fix snippets.           Does it actually ask ChatGPT about my site?

          No, the scanner checks the signals that make your site citable. But the "Test it yourself" panel gives you deep links to ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Google's AI search with a prefilled query about your domain, so you can see what they actually return — for free, in one click.

          Why not scan every page on my site?

          We sample up to 10 pages chosen by type (home, about, contact, a service page, a product page, an article, an FAQ, pricing). That gives a more accurate read on site-wide patterns than scanning 50 random URLs. We tell you exactly which pages we looked at.

          Is it really free with no catch?

          Yes. No signup, no email gate, no PDF email. Results live for 7 days at a share link. The whole tool runs on Cloudflare's free tier; the rubric is published openly on GitHub.
